## Quick-Start Checklist for Each Post

- [ ] H1 contains primary keyword
- [ ] Title tag under 60 characters, includes year if a roundup/pricing post
- [ ] Meta description under 155 characters, includes primary keyword and a hook
- [ ] Intro paragraph mentions primary keyword within first 100 words
- [ ] At least one internal link to a solution page or the /nebula-vs-ats page
- [ ] At least one CTA ("See how Nebula compares -- Book a demo")
- [ ] No em dashes
- [ ] Platform-first language ("Nebula is a recruiting platform that uses AI" not "Nebula's AI...")
- [ ] Does not claim Nebula replaces ATS -- always "works alongside"
