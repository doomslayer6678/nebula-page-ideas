## Style Guide (from existing Nebula pages)

Pulled directly from homepage.pdf and solutions page.pdf.

### Structure every page follows:
1. **Hero headline** — 4–8 words, punchy, outcome-first
2. **Sub-headline** — 1–2 sentences max, who it's for + what they get
3. **Primary CTA** — "Book a demo →"
4. **3 stats** — large number + short ALL CAPS label
5. **Section blocks** — each labeled with "• THE [LABEL]" in small caps
6. **Challenge block** — 2–3 sentences on the pain, 2 supporting stats
7. **How we help block** — checkmark bullet list, 4–5 items, single-line each
8. **Outcomes block** — 2–3 sentences on the result
9. **Social proof** — customer logos or testimonial quote + name/title
10. **Final CTA block** — "See Nebula in action / Book a 30-minute demo / Book a demo →"

### Tone rules:
- Active voice always
- Outcome before feature ("Fill roles faster" not "We have AI sourcing")
- No paragraphs longer than 2 sentences
- No jargon without immediate payoff
- Never say "leveraging" or "utilizing"
- Speak like a head of talent, not a tech founder (per CEO media training)

### Do NOT do:
- Long-form copy
- Feature laundry lists without context
- Leading with "AI" as the noun (platform first, AI as modifier)
- Saying "we replace your ATS" — always "works alongside"
