## • WHAT AN ATS DOES WELL

**Your ATS Is Great at Tracking Applicants. That's Exactly What It Was Built For.**

Applicant tracking systems do one thing well: they organize candidates who have already applied, manage hiring stages, and maintain compliance records. They're built for inbound. For the pipeline you already have. For recording what happened after someone raised their hand.

For that job, they work.

---

## • WHERE THE GAP IS

**The Jobs Your ATS Doesn't Do**

The best candidates for your open roles usually haven't applied. They're working elsewhere, not browsing job boards, and not in your ATS. Traditional applicant tracking doesn't find them — and it was never designed to.

✗ Finds passive candidates who haven't applied
✗ Automates personalized outreach at scale
✗ Surfaces real-time talent market intelligence
✗ Shows you why a role is taking 60 days to fill
✗ Identifies which sourcing channels are actually producing hires

## • HOW NEBULA FILLS THE GAP
