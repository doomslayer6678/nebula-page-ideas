## Immediate Fixes (No New Pages — Just Meta Updates)

These are existing pages ranking well but bleeding clicks due to bad CTR. Fix title tags and meta descriptions before building anything new.

### 1. /features — URGENT
**Stats:** Position 4.41, 3,943 impressions, **0.23% CTR** (9 clicks)
**Problem:** Position 4 with 0.23% CTR is broken. Industry average at pos 4 is ~8%. Something is wrong with the title/meta.
**Fix:** Rewrite title tag and meta description. Lead with outcomes ("Fill roles 4x faster"), include a strong CTA-like hook.

### 2. /freemium — HIGH PRIORITY
**Stats:** Position 7, 8,443 impressions, **0.62% CTR** (52 clicks)
**Problem:** Second-highest impression page after homepage but terrible CTR. Searchers are seeing it and not clicking.
**Fix:** Title should be "Try Nebula Free — AI Recruiting Platform (No Credit Card Required)" and meta should hook with outcomes.

### 3. /blog/gem-pricing-vs-nebula — HIGH PRIORITY
**Stats:** Position 7.47, 4,293 impressions, **0% clicks**
**Problem:** The highest-impression blog gets zero clicks. Something about the title/snippet is not compelling.
**Fix:** Update title tag to include year and a hook ("Gem Pricing vs Nebula (2026): The Real Cost Comparison").

### 4. /frequently-asked-questions — HIGH PRIORITY
**Stats:** Position 5.69, 995 impressions, **0 clicks**
**Problem:** Position 5 should be generating ~50+ clicks/month on 995 impressions. Zero means the title/meta is completely uncompelling or the snippet looks like a dead page.
**Fix:** Rewrite meta. Title: "Nebula FAQ — How AI Recruiting Works, Pricing & Getting Started"

### 5. /blog/is-linkedin-recruiter-worth-the-cost
**Stats:** Position 12.93, 3,469 impressions, 0 clicks
**Action:** Not a quick fix — needs to rank higher. But 734 combined LinkedIn pricing queries (above) suggest building a dedicated LinkedIn Recruiter cost hub would capture all of them.

## Core Messaging Framework (from CEO Media Training, May 6 2026)

Use across all new content.

**Lead positioning:** "An all-in-one recruitment platform that uses AI" — platform first, AI as modifier.

**Key proof points:**
- 4x more powerful per recruiter
- 2–5x more revenue per team
- Up to $500K gross profit per head / $2.5M revenue per head
- 60+ ATS integrations
- 200M+ candidate profiles
- Patented ByeBias® — predictive, not generative
- Built by recruiters for recruiters (FUBU)
- Nearly a decade in the space — older than any competitor

**ATS differentiation (Steven's best answer):** "An ATS is a passive tool — it records but doesn't act. Nebula has encoded intelligence and automation. It creates opportunity rather than just logging a post-mortem. It's a living, breathing machine."

**On replacing recruiters:** "Relationships are historically underappreciated by software. We always want a human in the loop — their knowledge and capabilities amplified by technology, not replaced by it." Always lead with "irreplaceable."

**On AI quality:** "Our system is predictive, not generative — working with real data, not creating fictitious candidates. Patented methodology for eliminating bias."

**Audience-specific frames:**
| Audience | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Staffing Agency | Revenue per recruiter, capacity, $2.5M per head |
| Corporate TA | Fragmentation, visibility, one system |
| Healthcare Staffing | Constrained labor markets, nuance beyond job titles |
| Healthcare Corporate | Compliance, predictive AI, no hallucinations |
