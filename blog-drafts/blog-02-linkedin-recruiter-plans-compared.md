# LinkedIn Recruiter Lite vs Business+ vs Corporate: Which Plan Do You Actually Need?

LinkedIn sells three different products under the "Recruiter" umbrella, and most buyers do not realize how different they are until they have already picked one. Lite is not just a smaller version of Business+. Business+ is not just a cheaper version of Corporate. Each tier was built for a fundamentally different use case, and choosing the wrong one means either overpaying for features your team will never use or spending the year working around limits that slow every search down.

Here is how to read the difference.

## What Does LinkedIn Recruiter Lite Actually Include?

LinkedIn Recruiter Lite is the entry-level product. It is designed for individual recruiters or very small teams doing occasional hiring, not full-time talent acquisition professionals running active pipelines.

With Lite, you get 30 InMail credits per month, access to LinkedIn's basic talent search filters, and a simple pipeline view for tracking candidates. You can see full profiles for anyone in your first or second-degree network, but profiles outside that network are limited. There is no team collaboration, no shared pipeline, no Boolean search operators, and no ATS integration.

The price is approximately $140 to $170 per month depending on billing frequency. That is a meaningful cost savings over the higher tiers, but the limitations are real. If you are sourcing consistently at any volume, you will hit the ceiling of 30 InMails in the first two weeks of the month and spend the rest of the month waiting.

## Where Does Lite Fall Short?

The 30 InMail credit limit is the primary constraint, but it is not the only one. Recruiter Lite does not support multi-recruiter seat sharing, so every person on the team needs their own separate license with no coordination features. Searches cannot be saved in a shared folder. Notes and activity do not sync across seats.

For anyone doing more than five to eight hires per year, or for any team larger than one person, Lite creates operational friction that negates the pricing advantage. The upgrade to Business+ almost always happens within a year for any team that takes recruiting seriously.

## What Does LinkedIn Recruiter Business+ Add?

LinkedIn Recruiter (the Business+ tier) is the most common plan for in-house recruiting teams at companies that are hiring actively but are not enterprise scale. It adds 150 InMail credits per month, full profile visibility across LinkedIn's entire network regardless of connection degree, and access to LinkedIn's Spotlights feature, which surfaces candidates who have recently changed jobs, are open to new opportunities, or have grown their network recently.

Business+ also includes saved search alerts (LinkedIn notifies you when new candidates match a saved search), team collaboration and shared pipeline features, and basic candidate management with notes and activity history. You can export limited candidate data in CSV format, though the export is capped.

Pricing sits at approximately $825 per month or $8,999 to $9,900 per year, and this is where most buyers plant their flag. The jump from Lite to Business+ is steep in price but dramatic in capability.

## When Is Business+ the Right Choice?

Business+ works well for in-house teams running 30 to 100 hires per year with LinkedIn as their primary sourcing channel. If your hiring is concentrated in professional-class roles where LinkedIn penetration is high, and you have one to five dedicated recruiters who source from LinkedIn daily, the per-seat cost can make sense.

Where it starts to strain is outreach volume. At 150 InMail credits per seat per month, and an industry-average response rate of 15 to 25 percent, you are looking at roughly 20 to 37 meaningful conversations per recruiter per month from InMail alone. For a 50-requisition load, that pipeline math is thin. Most Business+ users supplement with LinkedIn connection request automation through tools like [Kanbox](https://kanbox.io), which adds a layer of outreach capacity on top of the InMail credits included in the seat.

## What Does LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate Add?

LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate is enterprise-grade, and the differences from Business+ go beyond just InMail volume. Corporate includes Hiring Manager Seat access, which allows non-recruiters to log in with limited view access to review shortlisted candidates without consuming a full recruiter seat. This matters for large teams where hiring managers need visibility without full recruiter access.

Corporate also includes LinkedIn's full Recruiter System Connect integration with enterprise ATS platforms, expanded analytics, dedicated customer success management, and negotiated InMail packages that can significantly exceed the Business+ credit allocation. Pricing is not published and is negotiated directly with LinkedIn's enterprise sales team, but reported ranges typically start around $12,000 to $15,000 per seat per year, with volume discounts at 10+ seats.

For teams running 200+ hires per year with multiple hiring stakeholders, a complex ATS workflow, and a need for executive-level reporting, Corporate is not optional. It is the only tier that supports the operational complexity.

## What Do Teams Miss When They Compare Tiers?

The comparison most teams run is: how many InMails do I need, and which tier gets me there? That is a reasonable question, but it misses the more important evaluation: is InMail even the right outreach channel for my hiring volume?

LinkedIn InMail response rates have declined meaningfully over the past several years as candidates have become habituated to recruiter outreach on the platform. Many high-quality passive candidates receive 20 to 30 InMails per month and respond to almost none of them. The teams winning passive candidate outreach in 2026 are not the ones with the most InMail credits. They are the teams reaching candidates through multiple channels, with personalized outreach, at the right moment in a candidate's career cycle.

That is a workflow question that the Lite vs Business+ vs Corporate decision does not answer. Tools like [Hyperclapper](https://hyperclapper.com) and AI sourcing platforms are increasingly where teams build that multi-channel capability, either alongside LinkedIn Recruiter or instead of it.

## So Which LinkedIn Recruiter Plan Do You Actually Need?

If you are an individual recruiter doing fewer than 10 hires per year, Lite is a reasonable starting point. If you are an in-house team running consistent hiring volume, Business+ is the functional floor. If you are enterprise with a complex ATS workflow and multiple hiring stakeholders, Corporate is the only tier that fits.

But the more useful question is whether the LinkedIn Recruiter model as a whole matches your outreach strategy. If you are relying exclusively on InMail to reach passive candidates, you are working with a tool that has real structural limits on response rates and outreach volume. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between LinkedIn Recruiter Lite and LinkedIn Recruiter?**

LinkedIn Recruiter Lite is the entry-level product with 30 InMail credits per month, basic search filters, and no team collaboration features. LinkedIn Recruiter (often called Business+) is a significantly more capable product with 150 InMail credits, full network visibility, team collaboration, saved search alerts, and Spotlights filters. The price difference is roughly $700 per month per seat, and the capability difference is equally significant. Lite is for occasional hiring at low volume; Business+ is for active in-house recruiting teams.

**Can you share InMail credits across LinkedIn Recruiter seats?**

InMail credits are allocated per seat and do not transfer between seats on the same account. Each recruiter's 30 (Lite) or 150 (Business+) credits are tied to that individual seat and expire at the end of the month if unused. LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate accounts can sometimes negotiate shared credit pools, but this requires specific contract terms and is not a default feature of any tier.

**Is LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate worth the cost for enterprise teams?**

For enterprise teams running 200+ hires per year with a complex ATS workflow and multiple hiring managers who need access, Corporate is generally worth it because it is the only tier that actually supports the operational requirements. The ROI calculation depends on cost per hire: if LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate reduces time-to-fill by even a few days at enterprise scale, the math typically justifies the spend. The question is whether LinkedIn sourcing alone justifies the cost, or whether a broader AI sourcing platform that includes LinkedIn data would deliver higher ROI at a comparable or lower total cost.

**How do you upgrade from LinkedIn Recruiter Lite to Business+?**

You can request an upgrade directly through your LinkedIn Recruiter account settings or by contacting LinkedIn's sales team. Upgrading mid-contract typically means paying the prorated difference for the remainder of your current billing period. LinkedIn's sales team will often offer to honor your existing renewal date to avoid proration, so it is worth asking. Moving to Corporate requires a new enterprise sales conversation and will not happen through a self-serve flow.
