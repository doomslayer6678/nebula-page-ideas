# The Best ATS for Staffing Agencies in 2026, Ranked

Staffing agencies have different ATS needs than in-house corporate TA teams, and the platforms built for corporate hiring often fall short for agency workflows. Multi-client management, contractor tracking, time-and-expense integration, placement reporting, and candidate redeployment pipelines are staffing-specific requirements that most general-purpose ATS platforms were never designed to handle well.

This ranked list covers the ATS options that were actually built with staffing agencies in mind, plus what to look for when you are evaluating them and how a sourcing platform like Nebula fits on top of whichever system you choose.

## What Makes an ATS Good for Staffing Agencies Specifically?

Before the rankings, the criteria. A staffing agency ATS needs to handle candidate ownership differently from corporate ATS platforms, because agencies place the same candidates across multiple client accounts over time. Candidate records need to persist across placements, and the system needs to track which clients each candidate has been submitted to, hired by, or declined by.

Multi-client account management is the second structural requirement. Agency recruiters work on multiple client requisitions simultaneously, and the ATS needs to keep those pipelines cleanly separated while still giving the recruiter a unified view of their workload.

Contractor lifecycle management, including time tracking, payroll data handoff, and redeployment alerting when a placed contractor's end date approaches, is the third staffing-specific requirement that general-purpose ATS platforms typically handle poorly.

## Bullhorn

Bullhorn is the most widely adopted ATS in the staffing industry and has been for well over a decade. It was designed specifically for staffing agencies, not adapted from a corporate ATS, and its data model reflects that: candidate records are managed independently from job orders, clients are first-class objects in the system, and placement tracking is central to the workflow.

Bullhorn handles contract and direct placement, supports large agency organizations with complex permission structures, and has an extensive integration ecosystem covering time-and-expense, payroll, VMS, and job board distribution. The platform is not the most modern in terms of user experience, but the operational depth for staffing is hard to match.

Pricing is enterprise-negotiated and typically reflects the scale of the operation. Bullhorn is rarely the right choice for agencies under 10 recruiters given the cost and implementation complexity, but it is the default for mid-market and large staffing organizations.

According to reporting on the staffing technology market from [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com), Bullhorn commands the largest market share in the staffing ATS category and continues to invest in AI-assisted features through its Bullhorn Copilot product line.

## Avionte

Avionte is a staffing-specific platform that serves light industrial, clerical, and commercial staffing agencies particularly well. It combines ATS functionality with front-office and back-office operations in a single platform, which is a significant operational advantage for agencies that want to avoid system fragmentation across candidate management, time tracking, and payroll.

The front-to-back integration means that a placed candidate flows through the entire lifecycle inside one system, from application through time reporting through payroll processing, without manual data re-entry across platforms. For high-volume light industrial staffing with frequent contractor cycling, this integration is a material efficiency gain.

Avionte's candidate experience features, including a mobile app for worker onboarding and time submission, are competitive. It is less strong for professional and executive search use cases, where the higher-touch workflow does not fit Avionte's process-driven design.

## Loxo

Loxo is a newer entrant in the staffing ATS market that combines ATS functionality with a built-in sourcing database and CRM. For agencies that want to reduce their tool stack, Loxo's all-in-one positioning is appealing: sourcing, outreach, and pipeline management inside one platform without requiring separate subscriptions for each function.

The sourcing database is useful for professional staffing, and the outreach automation is functional. The tradeoff is depth: Loxo is broader than it is deep, and agencies with complex workflows often find the limitations of the ATS layer more constraining than a specialist platform like Bullhorn or Avionte would be.

Loxo is a strong option for agencies in the 5 to 20 recruiter range that want a simpler technology footprint and are willing to trade some operational depth for reduced tool-switching and integration overhead.

## TargetRecruit

TargetRecruit is a Salesforce-native ATS built for staffing, which means it runs on top of the Salesforce platform and inherits Salesforce's data model, reporting, and integration capabilities. For agencies that already use Salesforce for their business development and client relationship management, TargetRecruit is a natural extension that unifies the candidate side and the client side in a single data environment.

The Salesforce foundation gives TargetRecruit enterprise-grade customizability, reporting depth, and an extensive integration library. The tradeoff is that it requires Salesforce licensing in addition to TargetRecruit licensing, which increases total cost, and the combined system requires more administration than a standalone staffing ATS.

TargetRecruit is most appropriate for agencies at 20+ recruiters where the Salesforce ecosystem is already in place and the unified reporting across sales and delivery is a meaningful operational priority.

## Crelate

Crelate is designed for independent recruiters and smaller staffing firms. It combines ATS and CRM functionality in a clean interface at a price point more accessible than the enterprise platforms above. The sourcing and candidate management features are solid for the use case, and the learning curve is gentle enough for small teams that do not have dedicated ATS administrators.

For agencies under 10 recruiters running a mix of direct placement and contract staffing, Crelate is a strong starting point that does not require the implementation overhead of Bullhorn or the Salesforce infrastructure of TargetRecruit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best ATS for a staffing agency with under 10 recruiters?**

For agencies with fewer than 10 recruiters, Crelate and Loxo are typically the strongest starting points. Both offer a reasonable combination of ATS and CRM functionality at accessible price points, with lower implementation overhead than enterprise platforms like Bullhorn. Loxo adds a built-in sourcing database that reduces the need for separate sourcing tools at this stage. As the agency scales past 10 to 15 recruiters, most operations need to evaluate a move to a more robust platform.

**Does my staffing agency need both an ATS and a CRM?**

Most staffing agencies need functionality that spans both categories: ATS features for managing candidates through placement workflows, and CRM features for managing client relationships, job orders, and business development activity. Some platforms, like Bullhorn and TargetRecruit, combine both in a single system. Others, like Crelate and Loxo, provide a unified interface for both use cases. The question is whether you want a best-of-breed approach (separate best-in-class ATS and CRM) or a unified platform. For agencies under 20 recruiters, unified is usually the right starting point.

**How much does a staffing agency ATS cost?**

Staffing ATS pricing varies significantly. Entry-level platforms like Crelate typically run $99 to $199 per user per month. Mid-market platforms like Avionte and Loxo are typically in the $100 to $300 per user per month range depending on included features. Enterprise platforms like Bullhorn are custom-priced based on team size, contract length, and included modules, with mid-sized agency contracts typically running $30,000 to $100,000+ per year. TargetRecruit adds Salesforce licensing to the TargetRecruit cost, which increases the total significantly for teams that do not already have Salesforce.
